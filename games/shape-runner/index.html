Game description

Shape Runner is an arcade challenge where timing and shape-matching matter. Pick the right form, leap over hazards, and keep your streak alive.

  • Genre: Arcade
  • Best for: Fast reflexes and quick decisions
  • Mode: Endless runner

Controls

  • Space / Tap Jump
  • 1, 2, 3 or Shape Button to switch forms
  • P pauses the run
